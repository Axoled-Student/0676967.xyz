media/bad-apple.gz.txt
----------------------
The ASCII text frames for the auto-playing "ASCII Bad Apple!!" screen on the
home page (index.html, js/bad-apple.js) come from the generated file
`generated/play.txt` in:

  https://github.com/Sub0X/ASCII_Bad_Apple_Remastered
  (commit b5ec037, 2022-11-13)

Modifications: frames were split on the "SPLIT" marker, trailing whitespace
was removed from every line, "." characters were replaced with spaces (as the
original player does), the frames were joined with a form-feed (\f) character,
compressed with gzip and base64-encoded into a text file. The frame contents
are otherwise unchanged.
